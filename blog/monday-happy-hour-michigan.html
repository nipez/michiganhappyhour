Best Monday Happy Hours in Michigan (2026)

Updated November 2026 · MichiganHappyHour.com

Looking for a happy hour Monday near me in Michigan? Good news: Monday is when most bars actually run specials. From our published listings, 140+ spots include Monday with verified start and end times — classic Mon–Fri windows plus a few all-day and late-night Monday deals. Here’s a statewide roundup by town, with real hours and deals from the live directory.

Traverse City

Front Street and downtown TC lean hard into Monday — several spots run every day, and one turns Monday into an all-night pour.

The Parlor runs 3:00 PM–7:00 PM with 50% off all drinks and 25% off the full kitchen menu — plus all-night happy hour Mondays. Speakeasy-inspired cocktails with lively downtown energy.

Mama Lu’s Taco Shop runs 3:00 PM–6:00 PM with $7 classic margaritas, $5 apps, and $4 house wine. Laid-back taco spot with legendary $2 Taco Tuesdays all day.

The Little Fleet runs 4:00 PM–6:00 PM with $5 draft beer and $8 margaritas. Outdoor food-truck court and bar — a downtown TC institution.

Rare Bird Brewpub runs 3:00 PM–5:30 PM with $1 off all pints and half-off apps. Neighborhood brewpub with an outstanding patio.

Firefly runs 4:00 PM–7:00 PM with $8 craft cocktails and $6 wines. Warm neighborhood spot with seasonal New American fare.

Full list: Traverse City town page, TC region, and the Traverse City happy hour guide.

Ann Arbor

Ashley’s runs 3:00 PM–6:00 PM with $4 Michigan beer pints, $7 select cocktails, and $4 wells. Legendary State Street beer bar with an awe-inspiring tap list.

Zingerman’s Roadhouse runs 2:00 PM–6:00 PM with $2 off all 16oz drafts plus BBQ taco and oyster specials. An early Monday window if you’re off work early.

Aventura runs 3:00 PM–6:00 PM with $3 off wine by the glass and $10 martini, Manhattan, or Old Fashioned. Spanish-inspired oasis downtown.

HopCat Ann Arbor runs 3:00 PM–5:30 PM with 50% off select apps, $4 local drafts, and $6 mules & meowgaritas.

Old Town runs a late Monday window 10:00 PM–12:00 AM with 50% off select drinks and Monday $1 off Michigan alcohol — useful if the standard 3–6 window doesn’t fit.

Grand Rapids

Founders Brewing Co. runs 2:00 PM–6:00 PM with $2.50 off pints, $2 off flights, and $5 flatbread pizzas. Iconic craft brewery in a converted trucking depot.

Brick and Porter runs 2:00 PM–6:00 PM with $3 wells and $4 select calls. Reliable early Monday value downtown.

Carolina Lowcountry Kitchen runs 4:00 PM–6:00 PM with half-off domestic drafts, half-off house wine, and $2 fresh raw oysters.

The Apartment Lounge runs 2:00 PM–9:00 PM with Michigan Monday specials. Michigan’s longest-running LGBTQ+ bar — welcoming all with daily themed specials.

Grey Ghost runs 4:00 PM–6:00 PM with $8 cocktails, $5 draft beers, and $7 wine. Midtown staple with cheeseburger sliders and crispy pork belly on the happy hour board.

Anchor Bar runs 3:00 PM–6:00 PM with $5 well drinks, $8 Miller/PBR pitchers, and $3 sliders. Classic downtown dive value.

Bakersfield DET runs 4:00 PM–7:00 PM with $5 house margaritas, $3 Tecate cans, and $2 tacos.

Louie’s Trophy House runs 3:00 PM–6:00 PM with $3 house wines, $4 wells, and $5 appetizers.

New Holland Brewing runs 2:00 PM–6:00 PM with $2 off all pints and $1 off Dragon’s Milk 10oz. Holland’s flagship brewery happy hour.

Boatwerks Waterfront runs 3:00 PM–6:00 PM with $5 draft beers, $6 house wine, and $7 cocktails — sunset views included.

Pigeon Hill Brewing runs 3:00 PM–6:00 PM with $1 off all pints and $5 soft pretzels.

FAQ

Do most Michigan happy hours run on Monday?

Yes. Most published Michigan happy hours include Monday as part of a Monday–Friday window. From our live venue data, 140+ spots list Monday with verified start and end times — far more than Sunday.

Which Monday happy hours have all-day or late specials?

Standouts from current listings: The Parlor in Traverse City advertises all-night happy hour Mondays; Beards Brewery in Petoskey runs Monday $3 pints all day; Lake Bluff Grille in Muskegon lists all-day happy hour on Monday; Old Town in Ann Arbor runs a late 10:00 PM–12:00 AM window with Monday $1 off Michigan alcohol.

When do most Monday happy hours start in Michigan?

Most Monday windows start between 2:00 PM and 4:00 PM and wrap between 5:00 PM and 7:00 PM. A few stretch longer — The Apartment Lounge in Grand Rapids runs 2:00 PM–9:00 PM with Michigan Monday specials.
