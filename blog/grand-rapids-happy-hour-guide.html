The Ultimate Grand Rapids Happy Hour Guide (2026)

Updated August 2026 · MichiganHappyHour.com

Beer City USA earns the nickname at happy hour as much as it does at the festivals. Downtown, Wealthy Street, and the west side all run solid after-work deals, and several places open the window as early as 2 PM — rare in Michigan. We've got 109 Grand Rapids spots mapped with hours and specials. Here's the practical route.

Best Overall Deals

Stella's Lounge downtown is the value play: $2 PBR drafts, $3 wells, $5 nachos, and $5 off burgers from 4–7 PM. It's loud, casual, and reliably packed by 5:30 on Fridays — go earlier in the week if you want a booth without a wait.

Brick and Porter stretches the window earlier with $3 wells and $4 select calls from 2–6 PM Monday through Thursday — useful if you get out of work early or need a long runway before dinner. San Chez Bistro covers food and drinks with $2 off specialty cocktails and a $9 tapas menu from 2–5 PM, so you can actually eat a meal on the deal instead of just snacking.

Best Cocktails

One Bourbon is the whiskey-forward stop — $2 off house cocktails Tuesday through Friday from 4–6 PM. For a longer, themed run, The Apartment Lounge on Sheldon keeps specials going daily from 2–9 PM (Michigan Monday, Sangria Tuesday, Wine & Well Wednesday, Thirsty Thursday, plus a Sunday Bloody Mary bar). It's Michigan's longest-running LGBTQ+ bar and one of the most welcoming rooms in town.

If you're already downtown for dinner, Stella's wells and San Chez cocktails cover most of what you need without a second trip across the river. Save One Bourbon for a quieter Tuesday or Wednesday when you can sit at the bar and actually talk to the bartender.

Best for Beer

Start at Founders Brewing Co. — $2.50 off pints, $2 off flights, and $5 flatbread pizzas from 2–6 PM Monday through Friday. That early start is rare downtown and worth planning around, especially if you're hosting out-of-town beer people.

HopCat on Ionia runs $4 local drafts, $6 mules and meowgaritas, and $8 BYO burgers from 3–5:30 PM. Grand Rapids Brewing Co. is a short walk with $1 off select drafts and $2 retros & wells from 3–6 PM. On Wealthy Street, Archival Brewing does 25% off drinks and 15% off apps from 3–5 PM weekdays — one of the better percentage deals in the city, and the biergarten is the move when the weather cooperates. Between Founders, HopCat, GRBC, and Archival, you can build a full Beer City crawl without leaving the happy-hour clock.

Day-by-Day / Neighborhood Tips

Downtown core: Founders at 2 PM, then HopCat or GRBC before those windows close. Stella's and Brick and Porter fill the later afternoon if you're staying on the west side of the river. On Fridays, arrive at the open of happy hour or you'll lose an hour standing around.

Wealthy Street: Archival is the mid-week local play. Pair it with a walk toward downtown if you still want a second stop before six.

Thursday: Brick and Porter early, then One Bourbon or Stella's. Apartment Lounge's Thirsty Thursday specials run late enough that you don't have to race the clock.

Friday: Expect lines at Founders and Stella's. Shift to San Chez or Apartment Lounge if you want a steadier pace without losing the deal.

Weekend: Apartment Lounge's daily themes still apply Sunday through Saturday. Otherwise treat Saturday as a full-price beer pilgrimage and save the discounted pints for the workweek when Founders, HopCat, and Archival are actually discounted.
