The Ultimate Grand Rapids Happy Hour Guide (2026)

Updated November 2026 · MichiganHappyHour.com

Beer City USA earns the nickname at happy hour as much as it does at the festivals. Downtown, Wealthy Street, Bridge Street, and East Hills all run solid after-work deals, and several places open the window as early as 2 PM — rare in Michigan. We’ve got 110 Grand Rapids–region spots listed, including 20 with verified hours and specials highlighted below. Hours come from published listings — always confirm on the spot page before you go.

Best Overall Deals

Birch Lodge is the value headline right now: a $1.99 happy hour menu (food and drink items) from 3:00 PM–6:00 PM Monday–Friday. Pair it with Stella’s Lounge downtown — $2 PBR drafts, $3 wells, and $5 nachos from 4:00 PM–7:00 PM Monday–Thursday only.

Brick and Porter stretches earlier with $3 wells and $4 select calls from 2:00 PM–6:00 PM Monday–Wednesday (all-day Thursday specials are listed separately). San Chez Bistro covers food and drinks with half-off wines & drafts, $2 off specialty cocktails, and a $9 tapas menu from 2:00 PM–5:00 PM Monday–Friday.

The Green Well in East Hills goes half-off across the board — apps, drafts, house wines, and classic cocktails — from 3:00 PM–6:00 PM weekdays. Brickyard Tavern stretches to 7:00 PM with $4 taps and $5 off burgers.

Best Cocktails

One Bourbon is the whiskey-forward stop — $2 off house cocktails and half off wine & beer Tuesday through Saturday from 4:00 PM–6:00 PM (no published Monday window). For a longer, themed run, The Apartment Lounge on Sheldon keeps specials going daily from 2:00 PM–9:00 PM (Michigan Monday, Sangria Tuesday, Wine & Well Wednesday, Thirsty Thursday, plus a Sunday Bloody Mary bar). It’s Michigan’s longest-running LGBTQ+ bar and one of the most welcoming rooms in town.

ROAM by Sanchez near DeVos Hall publishes Tuesday–Thursday 3:00 PM–6:00 PM with half-off mules, margaritas, beer, and sangria. The Winchester on Wealthy lists $7 Winchester Mules and featured cocktails from 3:00 PM–6:00 PM weekdays. Bistro Bella Vita runs an American Apero window 3:00 PM–5:00 PM with $8 bistro martinis. Upscale and short: The Chop House lists 5:00 PM–6:00 PM Monday–Thursday with $7 select cocktails.

Best for Beer

Start at Founders Brewing Co. — $2.50 off pints, $2 off flights, and $5 flatbread pizzas from 2:00 PM–6:00 PM Monday–Thursday only. That early start is rare downtown and worth planning around midweek.

HopCat on Ionia runs $4 local drafts, $6 mules and meowgaritas, and $8 BYO burgers from 3:00 PM–5:30 PM — also Monday–Thursday only. Grand Rapids Brewing Co. is a short walk with $1 off select drafts and $2 retros & wells from 3:00 PM–6:00 PM Monday–Friday.

On Wealthy Street, Archival Brewing does 25% off drinks and 15% off apps from 3:00 PM–5:00 PM weekdays — one of the better percentage deals in the city, and the biergarten is the move when the weather cooperates. The Friesian Gastropub adds $1 off drafts nearby. Bridge Street’s Butcher’s Union lists $4.75 drafts and $8.50 cocktails from 4:00 PM–6:00 PM. Creston’s Graydon’s Crossing knocks $2 off draught beer in the same weekday window.

Oysters & Longer Windows

Most Grand Rapids happy hours wrap by 6:00 PM. If you want food that stretches the deal or a longer clock, these published listings stand out:

  • Carolina Lowcountry Kitchen — 4:00 PM–6:00 PM daily with half-off domestic drafts, half-off house wine, and $2 fresh raw oysters
  • The Apartment Lounge — 2:00 PM–9:00 PM daily with themed specials (Sangria Tuesday, Wine & Well Wednesday)
  • Brickyard Tavern — stretches to 7:00 PM Monday–Friday
  • Stella’s Lounge — 4:00 PM–7:00 PM Monday–Thursday
  • Founders / San Chez / Brick and Porter — early 2:00 PM opens

Pair an early Founders or San Chez stop with Stella’s or Apartment Lounge later instead of trying to chain three short windows on a busy night.

Day-by-Day / Neighborhood Tips

Downtown core: Founders at 2 PM (Monday–Thursday), then HopCat or GRBC before those windows close. Stella’s and Brick and Porter fill the later afternoon. On Fridays, Founders/HopCat/Stella’s do not publish happy hour — shift to San Chez, GRBC, Birch Lodge, or Green Well.

Wealthy Street: Archival, Winchester, and Friesian are the mid-week local play. Pair with a walk toward downtown if you still want a second stop before six.

Bridge Street: One Bourbon and Butcher’s Union cover whiskey and bites Tuesday onward. ROAM by Sanchez is the Tuesday–Thursday half-off cocktail option near DeVos.

Tuesday: Sangria Tuesday at Apartment Lounge, plus One Bourbon and ROAM opening their windows. Statewide Tuesday roundup: Tuesday happy hours.

Wednesday: Wine & Well Wednesday at Apartment Lounge; Founders/HopCat/Stella’s still inside Monday–Thursday. See the Wednesday happy hours guide.

Friday: Expect full-price crowds at Founders and Stella’s. Stick to venues that publish Friday — San Chez, Archival, Birch Lodge, Green Well, Brickyard, Carolina Lowcountry — or see the statewide Friday happy hours guide.

Weekend: Apartment Lounge’s daily themes and Carolina Lowcountry’s oyster window still apply. Otherwise treat Saturday as a full-price beer pilgrimage and save discounted pints for the workweek.

Friday after-work plan

Founders, HopCat, and Stella’s do not publish Friday happy hour. Build Friday around venues that do — using only the windows already listed above:

  • 2:00 PM early open: San Chez Bistro (2:00–5:00 PM Monday–Friday) — half-off wines & drafts, $2 off specialty cocktails, $9 tapas menu.
  • 3:00 PM beer & value block: Grand Rapids Brewing Co. (3:00–6:00 PM Monday–Friday), Archival Brewing on Wealthy (3:00–5:00 PM weekdays), Birch Lodge ($1.99 menu, 3:00–6:00 PM Monday–Friday), or The Green Well in East Hills (half-off apps, drafts, house wines, and classic cocktails, 3:00–6:00 PM weekdays).
  • Later finish: Brickyard Tavern stretches to 7:00 PM Monday–Friday with $4 taps and $5 off burgers. Or catch Carolina Lowcountry Kitchen 4:00–6:00 PM daily for half-off domestic drafts, half-off house wine, and $2 fresh raw oysters.

A simple downtown Friday: San Chez at 2:00, GRBC or Archival next, Brickyard until 7:00. Confirm each spot page before you go — hours can change.

FAQ

When do most Grand Rapids happy hours run?

Most published Grand Rapids windows start between 2:00 PM and 4:00 PM and wrap between 5:00 PM and 7:00 PM. Founders, San Chez Bistro, Brick and Porter, and The Apartment Lounge open as early as 2:00 PM. Stella’s Lounge stretches later (4:00 PM–7:00 PM, Monday–Thursday).

Do Founders, HopCat, and Stella’s run happy hour on Friday?

No. Current published listings show Founders Brewing Co., HopCat, and Stella’s Lounge as Monday–Thursday only. For Friday after-work deals, try San Chez Bistro, Grand Rapids Brewing Co., Archival Brewing, Birch Lodge, The Green Well, Brickyard Tavern, or Carolina Lowcountry Kitchen.

Where are the cheapest Grand Rapids happy hours?

Birch Lodge lists a $1.99 happy hour menu from 3:00 PM–6:00 PM Monday–Friday. Stella’s Lounge runs $2 PBR drafts and $3 wells Monday–Thursday 4:00 PM–7:00 PM. Brick and Porter lists $3 wells from 2:00 PM–6:00 PM Monday–Wednesday.

What are the best midweek (Tuesday/Wednesday) specials in Grand Rapids?

The Apartment Lounge runs Sangria Tuesday and Wine & Well Wednesday inside a daily 2:00 PM–9:00 PM window. One Bourbon and ROAM by Sanchez open their happy hour windows on Tuesday (not Monday). Founders, HopCat, and Stella’s are all inside their published Monday–Thursday windows midweek.
