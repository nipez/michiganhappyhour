Best Friday Happy Hours in Michigan (2026)

Updated November 2026 · MichiganHappyHour.com

Looking for a happy hour Friday near me in Michigan? From our published listings, 170+ spots include Friday with verified start and end times — classic after-work windows plus a few late and second-shift deals. Crowds hit earlier than midweek, and some big-name taprooms publish Monday–Thursday only, so this roundup sticks to venues that actually list Friday.

Detroit metro

Friday after-work in Detroit works best if you land at open. Eastern Market and Greektown stay friendlier than Corktown cocktail rooms once the dinner rush starts.

Bakersfield DET runs 4:00 PM–7:00 PM with $5 house margaritas, $3 Tecate cans, and $2 tacos — one of the longer Friday windows in the city.

Anchor Bar runs 3:00 PM–6:00 PM with $5 well drinks, $8 Miller/PBR pitchers, and $3 sliders. Historic downtown dive value if you’re leaving an office early.

Firebird Tavern runs 3:00 PM–6:00 PM with half-off beers & wine and $5 small plates. Strong pre-arena pick in Greektown.

Grey Ghost runs 4:00 PM–6:00 PM with $8 cocktails, $5 draft beers, and $7 wine. Brush Park staple — get there close to four on Fridays.

The Sugar House runs 5:00 PM–7:00 PM with $8 classic cocktails and $5 drafts. Corktown’s cocktail benchmark; seats go fast on Fridays.

The Library runs 4:00 PM–7:00 PM with $7 classic cocktails, $5 drafts, and happy hour oysters. A quieter book-lined alternative if Sugar House is slammed.

Parc Detroit runs 3:00 PM–6:00 PM with $9 craft cocktails and $8–$11 wines overlooking Campus Martius. The Skip in Belt Alley runs 4:00 PM–6:00 PM with $5 select beers and $6 wells if you want outdoor tiki energy.

Full list: Detroit town page, Detroit region, and the Detroit happy hour guide. Patio hunters can also check Detroit patio happy hours.

Ann Arbor

Ashley’s runs 3:00 PM–6:00 PM with $4 Michigan beer pints, $7 select cocktails, and $4 wells. State Street’s safest Friday beer landing.

Zingerman’s Roadhouse runs 2:00 PM–6:00 PM with $2 off all 16oz drafts plus BBQ taco and oyster specials — useful if you can leave work early.

Grizzly Peak Brewing runs 3:00 PM–6:00 PM with $1 off house pints and $5 select apps. Jolly Pumpkin runs 4:00 PM–6:00 PM with $4–$5 drafts and $10 pizzas.

The Last Word runs 5:00 PM–7:00 PM with $9 classic cocktails and $5 beer & wine. Smaller room — show up at five or save it for midweek.

Late Friday options: The Blue Leprechaun stretches 4:00 PM–8:00 PM with $4 Michigan pints and $5 wells; The Circ Bar runs a later 6:00 PM–9:00 PM window with discounted cocktails and rooftop patio vibes.

Grand Rapids

Note before you go: published listings for Founders Brewing Co., Stella’s Lounge, and HopCat Grand Rapids are Monday–Thursday only. These Friday-confirmed stops fill the gap.

San Chez Bistro runs 2:00 PM–5:00 PM with half-off wines & drafts, half-off sangria, and a $9 tapas menu — one of the earliest Friday starts downtown.

Carolina Lowcountry Kitchen runs 4:00 PM–6:00 PM with half-off domestic drafts, half-off house wine, and $2 fresh raw oysters.

Brickyard Tavern runs 3:00 PM–7:00 PM with $4 taps, $5 off burgers, and $2 off apps — a longer Friday window than most.

The Green Well runs 3:00 PM–6:00 PM with half-off appetizers, drafts, house wines, and classic cocktails.

One Bourbon runs 4:00 PM–6:00 PM Tuesday–Friday with half off wine & beer and $2 off house cocktails.

The Apartment Lounge runs 2:00 PM–9:00 PM daily — Michigan’s longest-running LGBTQ+ bar and one of the few rooms that keeps Friday specials going well past the usual 6 PM cutoff.

Traverse City

Front Street still runs strong on Fridays, and a few spots add late or longer windows.

The Parlor runs 3:00 PM–7:00 PM with 50% off all drinks and 25% off the full kitchen menu. Speakeasy-inspired cocktails downtown.

Mama Lu’s Taco Shop runs 3:00 PM–6:00 PM with $7 classic margaritas, $5 apps, and $4 house wine.

The Little Fleet runs 4:00 PM–6:00 PM with $5 draft beer and $8 margaritas. Outdoor food-truck court energy — arrive early on Fridays.

Rare Bird Brewpub runs 3:00 PM–5:30 PM with $1 off all pints and half-off apps. Firefly stretches 4:00 PM–7:00 PM with $8 craft cocktails and $6 wines.

Bubba’s runs 3:00 PM–6:00 PM with $3 craft drafts and half-off apps — plus a published late-night second window with $2 well drinks from 9 to close. One of the clearest Friday late plays in the state.

FAQ

Do most Michigan happy hours run on Friday?

Many do, but not all. From our live venue data, 170+ spots list Friday with verified start and end times. Some popular chains and brewery taprooms — including HopCat locations, Founders Brewing Co., and Stella’s Lounge — publish Monday–Thursday windows only, so always check the spot page before a Friday plan.

Which Friday happy hours run late or have a second late-night window?

Standouts from current listings: Bubba’s in Traverse City lists $2 well drinks late night 9–close in addition to its 3:00 PM–6:00 PM window; The Circ Bar in Ann Arbor runs 6:00 PM–9:00 PM; The Blue Leprechaun stretches to 8:00 PM; The Apartment Lounge in Grand Rapids keeps specials going 2:00 PM–9:00 PM.

When do most Friday happy hours start in Michigan?

Most Friday windows start between 3:00 PM and 4:00 PM and wrap between 5:30 PM and 7:00 PM. Arrive at the open on Fridays — downtown rooms in Detroit, Ann Arbor, and Grand Rapids fill earlier than midweek.

Where are the best after-work Friday happy hours in Detroit?

Strong Friday listings include Bakersfield DET (4:00 PM–7:00 PM with $2 tacos and $5 margs), Anchor Bar (3:00 PM–6:00 PM with $5 wells), Grey Ghost (4:00 PM–6:00 PM), Firebird Tavern (3:00 PM–6:00 PM), and The Sugar House (5:00 PM–7:00 PM). Parc Detroit and The Skip are solid if you want Campus Martius or Belt Alley energy.

How should I plan around Friday crowds?

Start earlier than you would midweek — many after-work rooms pack by 5:00 PM on Fridays. Pick one cocktail stop and one beer or taco stop instead of a three-bar crawl. If your usual spot is HopCat, Founders, or Stella’s, switch to a venue that publishes Friday hours, or save those for Monday–Thursday.
