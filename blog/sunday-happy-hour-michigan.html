Best Sunday Happy Hours in Michigan (2026)

Updated August 2026 · MichiganHappyHour.com

Most Michigan happy hours run Monday through Friday only — which makes Sunday planners feel left out. These spots list Sunday in their happy hour days with real deals, from Traverse City food-truck courts to Ann Arbor beer temples and Mackinac Island institutions.

Traverse City (every-day energy)

The Little Fleet runs 4:00 PM–6:00 PM with $5 draft beer. Laid-back outdoor food truck court and bar — a downtown TC institution.

Mama Lu’s Taco Shop runs 3:00 PM–6:00 PM with $7 classic margaritas. Laid-back taco spot with legendary $2 Taco Tuesdays all day.

Firefly runs 4:00 PM–7:00 PM with $8 craft cocktails. Warm neighborhood spot with seasonal New American fare.

Rare Bird Brewpub runs 3:00 PM–5:30 PM with $1 off all pints. Neighborhood brewpub with an outstanding patio.

Ann Arbor & Grand Rapids

Ashley’s runs 3:00 PM–6:00 PM with $4 Michigan beer pints. Legendary beer bar on State Street with an awe-inspiring tap list and scotch sel.

Aventura runs 3:00 PM–6:00 PM with $3 off wine by the glass. Spanish-inspired oasis downtown — Old World wines and tapas where lingering is a.

Mani Osteria runs 4:00 PM–6:00 PM with $5 prosecco. Italian bar and kitchen with a killer happy hour spritz and wood-fired pizza.

Carolina Lowcountry Kitchen runs 4:00 PM–6:00 PM with Half-off domestic drafts. Southern comfort meets Grand Rapids — fresh oysters for $2 at happy hour.

The Apartment Lounge runs 2:00 PM–9:00 PM with Michigan Monday specials. Michigan’s longest-running LGBTQ+ bar — welcoming all with daily themed specials. Sunday Bloody Mary bar is the draw.

Up North

Tap 30 Pourhouse runs 3:00 PM–6:00 PM with $2 off all taps. 30 taps of Michigan craft beer in downtown Charlevoix.

Horn’s Gaslight Bar runs 3:00 PM–5:30 PM with $6 island cocktails. Island institution since 1933 — the oldest bar on Mackinac.

Gate House at Mission Point runs 4:00 PM–6:00 PM with $8 craft cocktails. Upscale island dining with sweeping Straits views.

Sunday Planning Tips

Ashley’s and The Little Fleet are the safest Sunday bets when you don’t want to call ahead. For island trips, Horn’s Gaslight and Gate House both run daily windows — factor in ferry time so you’re seated before specials end.

Most Michigan bars still skip Sunday entirely. When in doubt, check our map or city guides: Ann Arbor, Grand Rapids, Traverse City.
