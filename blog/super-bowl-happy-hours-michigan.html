Super Bowl Happy Hours & Watch Parties in Michigan

Updated November 2026 · MichiganHappyHour.com

Super Bowl Sunday is Michigan’s biggest bar day of the winter — and the day our directory is least likely to have a special one-off menu on file. This guide lists sports bars and taverns with verified happy hour hours from published data, then gives evergreen tips for finding wing deals, early opens, and ticketed watch parties without inventing specials we cannot confirm.

How to plan Super Bowl Sunday (evergreen)

  • Weekday windows are not game-day menus. Use them to shortlist TV-friendly bars, then call for Sunday hours, reservations, and food specials.
  • Arrive before kickoff. Even when a bar runs afternoon specials, seats vanish an hour before the national anthem.
  • Check Sunday coverage. Many Mon–Fri happy hours do not run Sunday — see our Sunday happy hours guide for spots that publish weekend windows.
  • Ticketed parties happen. If a venue website advertises a Super Bowl package, that usually replaces the normal happy hour board.

Sports bars & taverns with verified hours

These listings combine sports-bar/tavern energy with published start/end times and deals:

  • Art’s Pub (Lansing) — 3:00 PM–6:00 PM: $4 wells, $1 off drafts, half-off apps. Legendary Lansing sports bar.
  • Sidebar East Lansing — 3:00 PM–6:00 PM: $5 cocktails, $4 drafts, bar snack specials near MSU.
  • Frick’s Sports Bar (Midland) — 3:00 PM–6:00 PM: $1 off all drinks, $5 loaded nachos, wing specials.
  • Brickyard Tavern (Grand Rapids) — 3:00 PM–7:00 PM Monday–Friday: $4 taps, $5 off burgers — a longer weekday window for early watch parties the week of the game.
  • Firebird Tavern (Detroit) — 3:00 PM–6:00 PM Monday–Friday: half-off beers & wine, $5 small plates in Greektown.
  • Westgate Tavern & Grill (Lansing) — 3:00 PM–6:00 PM: daily drink specials, $1 wings Wednesday.
  • Stucko’s Pub & Grill (Marquette) — 3:00 PM–6:00 PM: $1 off pints, $5 burger & fries combo.
  • Zoobie’s Old Town Tavern (Lansing) — 3:00 PM–6:00 PM: $2 PBR, $4 well highballs, $3–$6 apps.
  • Merl’s Tavern (Saginaw) — 3:00 PM–7:00 PM: $3 wells, $2 PBR cans, $1 off drafts.
  • Timbers Bar & Grill (Saginaw) — 3:00 PM–7:00 PM: $4 wells, $3 domestic drafts, half-off apps.

City starting points

Detroit: Pair Firebird with downtown anchors from the Detroit guide — Anchor Bar (3:00–6:00 PM Mon–Fri: $5 wells, $8 pitchers) is a classic pre-game value stop when the big game falls on a travel weekend.

Grand Rapids: Use the Grand Rapids guide and Brickyard as a weekday warm-up; for Sunday kickoff, call sports-forward rooms and confirm TV sound.

Lansing / East Lansing: Art’s, Sidebar, Zoobie’s, and Westgate give you a dense shortlist — see the Lansing guide.

Tri-Cities: Merl’s, Timbers, Frick’s, and Harvey’s Grill & Bar cover Saginaw–Midland game-day energy — Tri-Cities guide.

When happy hour doesn’t cover kickoff

Super Bowl kickoff is almost always outside the classic 3–6 PM window. Practical moves:

  1. Use happy hour for a pre-game pour (Saturday night or early Sunday if the bar publishes a weekend window).
  2. Open the spot page for phone/website and ask about wing pitches, pitcher deals, or cover charges.
  3. Fall back to the Sunday happy hours roundup and the live map filtered by your city.

FAQ

Do Michigan bars publish Super Bowl-only happy hours?

Most listings publish regular weekday or daily windows, not Super Bowl-only menus. Shortlist sports bars with verified hours, then call or check the venue website for ticketed watch parties and wing specials.

Which Michigan sports bars have verified happy hour hours?

Examples: Art’s Pub, Frick’s, Sidebar East Lansing, Brickyard Tavern, and Firebird Tavern. Confirm TVs and Sunday hours on the spot page.
