Michigan Happy Hour Laws: What You Need to Know in 2026

Updated March 2026 · MichiganHappyHour.com

Michigan is one of the friendlier states when it comes to happy hour laws. Unlike some states that ban discounted drink promotions entirely, Michigan allows bars and restaurants to offer reduced prices on alcoholic beverages during designated hours. Here's what you need to know as a consumer — or as a bar owner planning your promotions.

The Basics: What's Allowed

Michigan law permits establishments with liquor licenses to offer happy hour drink specials, including reduced-price cocktails, beer, and wine. This is great news for both bar owners looking to draw in after-work crowds and for customers looking for a deal on their favorite craft cocktails.

Bars can set their own happy hour schedules, and there's no state-mandated restriction on which days or times happy hours can occur. This is why you'll find everything from traditional weekday 3–6 PM windows to all-day Monday specials and late-night deals.

What's Not Allowed

While Michigan is permissive with happy hour specials, there are some important restrictions. The Michigan Liquor Control Commission prohibits certain practices designed to encourage over-consumption. These include unlimited drink offers for a fixed price ("all you can drink" specials), increasing the amount of alcohol in a drink without increasing the price, and serving more than two drinks to a single customer at one time.

Additionally, establishments cannot offer free drinks as an inducement to purchase other drinks or food. The bottom line: deals are fine, but promotions that encourage binge drinking are not.

Food Requirements

Michigan does not require bars to serve food alongside discounted drinks, though many establishments choose to pair drink deals with appetizer specials. This is actually a smart business move — our data shows that happy hours offering both food and drink deals get significantly more engagement on MichiganHappyHour.com.

Regional Variations

While state law provides the framework, local ordinances can add additional restrictions. Some municipalities may have noise ordinances or patio service hours that effectively limit when outdoor happy hours can operate. If you're a bar owner, it's worth checking with your local clerk's office to understand any municipal-level restrictions in your area.

Advertising Happy Hours

Michigan allows establishments to advertise their happy hour specials, including posting prices and times on social media, websites, and signage. This is another area where Michigan is more permissive than some states. That's exactly why a resource like MichiganHappyHour.com can exist — we help connect deal-seekers with the best specials across the state.

Tips for Getting the Most Out of Happy Hour

Michigan's happy hour scene is thriving, with over 180 spots listed on our guide alone. To get the most out of it, consider arriving right when happy hour starts for the best seating, checking if the establishment offers food specials alongside drink deals, looking for spots with extended happy hours (some run until 7 PM or later), and exploring brewery taprooms, which often have the best value per pint.
