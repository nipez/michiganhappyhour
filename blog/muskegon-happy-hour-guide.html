The Ultimate Muskegon Happy Hour Guide (2026)

Updated August 2026 · MichiganHappyHour.com

Muskegon’s after-work scene runs from lake-view brewery patios to Pere Marquette beach decks and downtown cocktail rooms. We’ve got 24 Muskegon-area spots mapped — here are the ones with verified hours worth planning around.

Best Brewery Deals

Pigeon Hill Brewing runs 3:00 PM–6:00 PM with $1 off all pints, $2 off flights, $5 soft pretzels. Muskegon's beloved brewery with lake views and the legendary Renny IPA.

Unruly Brewing runs 3:00 PM–6:00 PM with $1 off house beers, $3 off pitchers, smoked meat specials. Downtown brewery and live music venue with a social-district to-go cup.

North Grove Brewers runs 3:00 PM–6:00 PM with $1 off pints, $3 off growler fills. Hole-in-the-wall brewpub energy with arcade games, darts, and a biergarten.

Best Downtown Cocktails

Liquid Assets runs 4:00 PM–6:00 PM with $2 off craft cocktails, $5 wine pours, $4 select beers. Stylish bar inside a converted bank lobby — Aviation cocktails and French 75s.

18th Amendment Spirits Co. runs 4:00 PM–6:00 PM with $2 off craft cocktails, $5 classics, small plates. Prohibition-themed speakeasy downtown with handcrafted drinks.

46 Bar runs 3:00 PM–6:00 PM with $1 off all drinks, discounted appetizers, famous burger specials. Muskegon's go-to for burgers, pull tabs, and a big outdoor patio.

Best Waterfront / Beach

The Deck runs 3:00 PM–5:00 PM with $2 off drafts, $5 BBQ sliders, seasonal drink specials. The only beachfront restaurant on Pere Marquette Beach — tiki vibes and Lake Michigan sunsets.

Lake Bluff Grille runs 3:00 PM–5:30 PM with $2 off drafts & seltzers, discounted appetizers, all-day happy hour Monday. New American dining with Muskegon Lake views.

Best All-Day / Neighborhood

Hank's Tavern runs half-off draft beer all day Tuesday and 25% off drinks all day Thursday. Classic family restaurant with all-day drink deals — no tight window required.

Pints & Quarts runs 3:00 PM–6:00 PM with quarter-off appetizers Thursday, daily food specials, BOGO burgers Monday. Neighborhood bar & grill beloved since 2003.

How to Plan the Night

Start at Pigeon Hill or Unruly for brewery deals, swing downtown for Liquid Assets if you want cocktails, then catch The Deck when the weather cooperates. Mondays at Lake Bluff stretch the window all day — useful after a long shift.
