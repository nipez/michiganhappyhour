The Ultimate Holland Happy Hour Guide (2026)

Updated August 2026 · MichiganHappyHour.com

Holland packs a lot into a walkable downtown: flagship brewery deals, HopCat apps, Irish pints on 8th Street, and Lake Macatawa sunsets if you want a waterfront window. We’ve got 20 Holland spots mapped — start with these verified hours and specials.

Best Brewery Deals

New Holland Brewing runs 2:00 PM–6:00 PM with $2 off all pints, $1 off Dragon's Milk 10oz, $8 Dragon's Milk Old Fashioned, $5 call drinks. Holland's flagship brewery — Dragon's Milk on draft and craft spirits downtown.

Big Lake Brewing runs 3:00 PM–6:00 PM with $1 off pints, $2 off flights, appetizer specials. Friendly neighborhood brewery with small-batch craft beers and hearty pub fare.

HopCat Holland runs 3:00 PM–5:30 PM with 50% off select apps, $4 local drafts, $6 mules & meowgaritas. Downtown craft beer destination with Cosmik Fries and a Thursday burger deal.

Best Downtown / 8th Street

Hops at 84 East runs 3:00 PM–6:00 PM with $1 off craft pints, $5 apps, half-off select pizzas. Lively pub fare right on 8th Street with a patio that fills fast in summer.

The Curragh runs 4:00 PM–6:00 PM with $5 Irish pints, $6 whiskey specials, $5 appetizers. Authentic Irish pub energy with whiskey and live music nights.

CitySen Lounge runs 4:00 PM–7:00 PM with $6 cocktails, $4 draft beer, daily food specials. Stylish hotel lounge with one of the longer windows in town.

Seventy Six runs 4:00 PM–6:00 PM with $7 cocktails, $5 beer & wine, discounted appetizers. Upscale downtown cocktails when you want something more polished.

Best Waterfront / Upscale

Boatwerks Waterfront runs 3:00 PM–6:00 PM with $5 draft beers, $6 house wine, $7 cocktails. Lake Macatawa sunset happy hour — hard to beat for views.

Butch's Dry Dock runs 4:00 PM–6:00 PM with $6 select wines, $5 craft drafts, appetizer specials. Fine dining meets craft beer and wine for a refined after-work stop.

Waverly Stone runs 4:00 PM–6:00 PM with $8 cocktails, $5 beer & wine, half-off select starters. Gastropub cocktails in a warm stone-walled space.

How to Plan the Night

Start early at New Holland (opens the window at 2), walk 8th Street for HopCat or Hops at 84 East, then catch Boatwerks if the weather’s right. Summer weekends fill patio seats first — arrive at the open.
