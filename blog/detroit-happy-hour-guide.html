The Ultimate Detroit Happy Hour Guide (2026)

Updated November 2026 · MichiganHappyHour.com

Detroit’s happy hour scene stretches from Corktown speakeasies to Midtown taprooms, Greektown taverns, and Campus Martius patios — and the deals are better than most visitors expect. Most weekday windows run roughly 3–7 PM, with cocktail bars skewing later and beer spots opening earlier. We’ve got 415 Detroit-region spots listed, including 20+ with verified hours and specials highlighted below. Here’s how to work the after-work circuit without guessing.

Best Overall Deals

Bakersfield DET in Eastern Market is hard to beat for value: $2 tacos, $5 house margaritas, $3 Tecate cans, and $5 queso from 4:00 PM–7:00 PM Monday–Friday. It’s one of the longer weekday windows in the city, so you can land after a late meeting and still catch the specials.

Green Dot Stables pairs creative $2.50–$5 sliders with $3 Michigan drafts and $4 wells from 3:00 PM–6:00 PM — the classic Detroit “eat cheap, drink local” combo. Anchor Bar downtown runs $5 well drinks, $8 Miller/PBR pitchers, $8 garbage can nachos, and $3 sliders from 3:00 PM–6:00 PM Monday–Friday. Firebird Tavern in Greektown lists half-off beers & wine with $5 small plates from 3:00 PM–6:00 PM Monday–Friday. If you want one stop that covers both food and drinks without stretching the wallet, start there before a game or a show downtown.

Dragonfly in Midtown publishes an inclusive $5 happy hour from 5:00 PM–7:00 PM — $5 menu items, $5 beer & shot combos, and $5 mocktails. The Block nearby runs a $6 happy hour menu (famous salmon sliders, $6 classic margarita) from 4:00 PM–6:00 PM Monday–Friday.

Best Cocktails

The Sugar House in Corktown is still the city’s cocktail benchmark — $8 classics and $5 drafts from 5:00 PM–7:00 PM Monday–Friday. Get there close to five; seats go fast on Thursdays and Fridays, and the bar fills before the kitchen dinner rush next door.

Downtown, Standby in the Belt serves $8 select cocktails and $5 beer specials Tuesday through Friday from 5:00 PM–7:00 PM. It’s a short walk from Campus Martius if you’re coming from an office. For something quieter and book-lined, The Library does $7 classics, $5 drafts, and happy hour oysters from 4:00 PM–7:00 PM Monday–Friday. Grey Ghost in Brush Park rounds it out with $8 cocktails, $5 drafts, $7 wine, and cheeseburger sliders from 4:00 PM–6:00 PM.

The Peterboro in historic Chinatown lists $6 craft cocktails and $4 beers Tuesday–Friday from 5:00 PM–7:00 PM, plus scallion pancake specials. Parc Detroit on Campus Martius runs $9 craft cocktails and $8–$11 wines from 3:00 PM–6:00 PM Monday–Friday — Detroit’s most scenic social hour. Besa adds rotating craft cocktails and a discounted raw bar from 4:00 PM–6:00 PM if you want Adriatic-inspired bistro energy.

Best for Beer & Outdoor

HopCat Detroit in Midtown brings the usual HopCat punch: 50% off select apps, $4 drafts, and $6 specialty drinks from 3:00 PM–5:30 PM — but published days are Monday–Thursday only (Thursday also lists a $9 burger + fries + draft deal). With 130 taps, it’s still the easiest place to sample Michigan beer midweek.

For neighborhood brewing, Batch Brewing Company in Corktown knocks $1 off pints and $2 off flights from 4:00 PM–6:00 PM Monday–Friday. Detroit Shipping Company is another Midtown option with $5 drafts and $6 cocktails in the same window — good when you want outdoor seating and a food-hall vibe. The Skip hides outdoor tiki energy in Belt Alley with $5 select beers and $6 wells from 4:00 PM–6:00 PM Monday–Friday. Wine drinkers shouldn’t skip Motor City Wine in Corktown — $6 select wines and $4 drafts every day from 5:00 PM–7:00 PM, often with live music.

Day-by-Day / Neighborhood Tips

Corktown: Sugar House, Batch, and Motor City Wine sit within a few blocks. Monday through Wednesday you can walk all three without a wait; Thursday and Friday, pick one cocktail bar and one beer stop instead of trying to hit everything.

Midtown / Brush Park: Detroit Shipping Company, Dragonfly, and The Block cover casual food and $5–$6 menus. Grey Ghost is the date-night upgrade a few minutes away. Save HopCat for Monday–Thursday and aim for its 3:00 PM start so you don’t miss the 5:30 cutoff.

Downtown / Campus Martius / the Belt: Parc for the view, Standby and The Skip for Belt Alley cocktails and tiki, Anchor Bar for cheap wells before a show.

Eastern Market & Greektown: Bakersfield for tacos and margs after market errands; Firebird when you’re heading to Little Caesars Arena or staying downtown. Both run Monday–Friday.

Monday–Wednesday: Lightest crowds. Hit Green Dot or Firebird early, then walk Corktown for Sugar House or Motor City Wine while bartenders still have time to talk.

Friday: Expect lines at Sugar House and Grey Ghost. Start at Bakersfield or Anchor Bar at open, then one cocktail stop — or see our statewide Friday happy hours guide.

Weekend: Motor City Wine’s daily window still works. Otherwise treat Saturday as a patio and shipping-container kind of night at Detroit Shipping Company or The Skip, and save the discounted cocktail bars for the workweek when the specials actually run.

FAQ

When do most Detroit happy hours run?

Most published Detroit windows start between 3:00 PM and 5:00 PM and wrap between 6:00 PM and 7:00 PM. Cocktail bars like The Sugar House and Standby skew later (5:00 PM–7:00 PM); beer and taco spots like Green Dot Stables, Firebird Tavern, and Anchor Bar often open at 3:00 PM.

Does HopCat Detroit run happy hour on Friday?

No. Current published listings show HopCat Detroit happy hour Monday–Thursday only, 3:00 PM–5:30 PM. For Friday after-work deals, try Bakersfield DET, Anchor Bar, Grey Ghost, Firebird Tavern, or The Sugar House instead.

Where are the best cheap Detroit happy hours?

Bakersfield DET lists $2 tacos, $5 house margaritas, and $3 Tecate cans from 4:00 PM–7:00 PM. Green Dot Stables runs $2.50–$5 sliders and $3 Michigan drafts from 3:00 PM–6:00 PM. Anchor Bar offers $5 wells, $8 pitchers, and $3 sliders from 3:00 PM–6:00 PM. Dragonfly Midtown publishes a $5 menu, $5 beer & shot, and $5 mocktails from 5:00 PM–7:00 PM.

What are the best cocktail happy hours in Detroit?

The Sugar House in Corktown lists $8 classics and $5 drafts from 5:00 PM–7:00 PM Monday–Friday. Standby in the Belt runs $8 select cocktails Tuesday–Friday 5:00 PM–7:00 PM. The Library offers $7 classics and $5 drafts from 4:00 PM–7:00 PM. Grey Ghost in Brush Park runs $8 cocktails, $5 drafts, and $7 wine from 4:00 PM–6:00 PM. The Peterboro adds $6 craft cocktails Tuesday–Friday 5:00 PM–7:00 PM.
