The Ultimate Detroit Happy Hour Guide (2026)

Updated August 2026 · MichiganHappyHour.com

Detroit's happy hour scene stretches from Corktown speakeasies to Midtown taprooms and Greektown taverns — and the deals are better than most visitors expect. Most weekday windows run roughly 3–7 PM, with cocktail bars skewing later and beer spots opening earlier. We've got 20 Detroit spots listed with real hours and specials. Here's how to work the after-work circuit without guessing.

Best Overall Deals

Bakersfield DET in Eastern Market is hard to beat for value: $2 tacos, $5 house margaritas, and $3 Tecate cans from 4–7 PM. It's one of the longer weekday windows in the city, so you can land after a late meeting and still catch the specials.

Pair that with Green Dot Stables, where $2.50–$5 creative sliders and $3 Michigan drafts run 3–6 PM — the classic Detroit “eat cheap, drink local” combo. Firebird Tavern in Greektown runs half-off drinks with $5 small plates from 3–6 PM Monday through Friday. If you want one stop that covers both food and drinks without stretching the wallet, start there before a game or a show downtown.

Best Cocktails

The Sugar House in Corktown is still the city's cocktail benchmark — $8 classics and $5 drafts from 5–7 PM Monday through Friday. Get there close to five; seats go fast on Thursdays and Fridays, and the bar fills before the kitchen dinner rush next door.

Downtown, Standby in the Belt serves $8 select cocktails and $5 beer specials Tuesday through Friday from 5–7 PM. It's a short walk from Campus Martius if you're coming from an office. For something quieter and book-lined, The Library does $7 classics and $5 drafts from 4–7 PM. Grey Ghost in Brush Park rounds it out with $8 cocktails, $5 drafts, and $7 wine from 4–6 PM — solid if you're already Midtown-adjacent and want elevated comfort food with the pour.

Best for Beer

HopCat Detroit in Midtown brings the usual HopCat punch: $4 drafts, $6 specialty drinks, and a $9 burger + fries + draft deal from 3–5:30 PM. With 130 taps, it's the easiest place to sample Michigan beer without hopping neighborhoods.

For neighborhood brewing, Batch Brewing Company in Corktown knocks $1 off pints and $2 off flights from 4–6 PM. Detroit Shipping Company is another Midtown option with $5 drafts and $6 cocktails in a similar window — good when you want outdoor seating and a food-hall vibe. Wine drinkers shouldn't skip Motor City Wine in Corktown — $6 select wines and $4 drafts every day from 5–7 PM, often with live music.

Day-by-Day / Neighborhood Tips

Corktown: Sugar House, Batch, and Motor City Wine sit within a few blocks. Monday through Wednesday you can walk all three without a wait; Thursday and Friday, pick one cocktail bar and one beer stop instead of trying to hit everything.

Midtown / Brush Park: HopCat and Detroit Shipping Company cover beer and casual food. Grey Ghost is the date-night upgrade a few minutes away. Aim for 3–4 PM starts at HopCat so you don't miss the 5:30 cutoff.

Eastern Market & Greektown: Bakersfield for tacos and margs after market errands; Firebird when you're heading to Little Caesars Arena or staying downtown. Both are stronger Monday–Thursday than Friday night.

Monday–Wednesday: Lightest crowds. Hit Green Dot or Firebird early, then walk Corktown for Sugar House or Motor City Wine while bartenders still have time to talk.

Weekend: Motor City Wine's daily window still works. Otherwise treat Saturday as a patio and shipping-container kind of night at Detroit Shipping Company, and save the discounted cocktail bars for the workweek when the specials actually run.
