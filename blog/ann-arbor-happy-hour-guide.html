The Ultimate Ann Arbor Happy Hour Guide (2026)

Updated August 2026 · MichiganHappyHour.com

Ann Arbor packs campus dive deals, downtown cocktail bars, and serious brewery happy hours into a few walkable blocks. Campus bars start early (often 2 PM); cocktail rooms downtown lean 5–7 PM. With 20 spots in our guide, you can cover Main Street, Liberty, and State Street in one evening — if you time the windows right. Here's the local playbook.

Best Overall Deals

The Brown Jug near campus is still the cheapest reliable window: $3 Coors/Miller/Labatt 22oz drafts, $5 Long Islands, and $4 premium pints from 2–4 PM. Get there early; the student rush hits hard after class, and the deal ends before most downtown bars even start.

Jolly Pumpkin downtown balances beer and food with $4 North Peak drafts, $5 Jolly Pumpkin drafts, and $10 pizzas from 4–6 PM. Ashley's is another Main Street staple — $4 Michigan beer pints, $7 select cocktails, $4 wells, and $5 wines from 3–6 PM Monday through Sunday. That seven-day schedule makes Ashley's the easiest fallback when midweek plans fall apart.

Best Cocktails

The Last Word is A2's cocktail destination: $9 classics and $5 beer & wine Tuesday through Friday from 5–7 PM. It's a smaller room — show up at five if you want a seat at the bar, especially on Thursdays.

The Ravens Club leans more supper-club, with $2 off appetizers during the 4–6 PM weekday window and a serious whiskey and cocktail list once you're settled. For a more casual downtown pour, Ashley's cocktail specials cover the middle ground without the speakeasy wait. If you're meeting friends who want beer and cocktails in the same stop, Ashley's or Jolly Pumpkin will keep everyone happier than bouncing between bars.

Best for Beer

HopCat Ann Arbor brings $4 local drafts, $6 mules and meowgaritas, and $6 wine from 3–5:30 PM — the standard HopCat happy hour, and one of the widest tap lists in town. Mothfire Brewing knocks $1 off pints and $2 off flights from 3–6 PM weekdays if you want a true brewery room instead of a taproom chain.

Grizzly Peak Brewing downtown does $1 off house pints, $5 select apps, and $6 wine by the glass from 3–6 PM. South of campus, Zingerman's Roadhouse runs $2 off all 16oz drafts Monday through Friday from 2–6 PM — worth the short drive if you're already headed that way for dinner. Late night, Old Town takes $1 off Michigan alcohol Sunday and Monday from 10 PM to midnight when most other deals are long gone.

Day-by-Day / Neighborhood Tips

Campus / State Street: Brown Jug owns the early afternoon. HopCat is the next stop if you still have time before 5:30. On football Saturdays, expect everything near campus to be slammed — shift downtown or go earlier.

Main Street downtown: Ashley's, Grizzly Peak, Jolly Pumpkin, Last Word, and Ravens Club all sit in a tight cluster. Monday through Wednesday you can walk two or three; Thursday and Friday, pick a beer spot at three and a cocktail bar at five.

Thursday: Last Word opens its cocktail window at five — plan around that. Pair with Jolly Pumpkin pizza if you want dinner in the same stretch of downtown.

Friday: Main Street fills early. Ashley's and Grizzly Peak are safer bets; save Last Word for earlier in the week if you hate standing.

Weekend / late night: Brown Jug's afternoon window still works when school's in session. Ashley's seven-day happy hour is the Saturday safety net. Old Town's late Michigan alcohol deal is the Sunday–Monday closer after everything else has flipped to full price.
