The Ultimate Ann Arbor Happy Hour Guide (2026)

Updated November 2026 · MichiganHappyHour.com

Ann Arbor packs campus dive deals, downtown cocktail bars, and brewery happy hours into a few walkable blocks. Campus bars often start early (2–3 PM); cocktail rooms downtown lean 5–7 PM. We’ve got 70 Ann Arbor–region spots listed, including 21 with verified hours and specials highlighted below. Hours come from published listings — always confirm on the spot page before you go.

Best Overall Deals

The Brown Jug near campus is still the cheapest reliable early window: $3 Coors/Miller/Labatt 22oz drafts, $5 Long Islands, $4 premium pints, and half-off appetizers from 2:00 PM–4:00 PM Monday–Thursday. Get there early; the student rush hits hard after class, and the deal ends before most downtown bars even start.

Ashley’s is the Main Street safety net — $4 Michigan beer pints, $7 select cocktails, $4 wells, and $5 wines from 3:00 PM–6:00 PM Monday–Friday and Sunday (no published Saturday window). Jolly Pumpkin downtown balances beer and food with $4 North Peak drafts, $5 Jolly Pumpkin drafts, and $10 pizzas from 4:00 PM–6:00 PM Monday–Friday.

Good Time Charley’s near campus runs 25% off all drinks & apps from 3:00 PM–5:00 PM Tuesday–Friday. Sava’s lists $5 select cocktails, $4 draft beer, and half-off appetizers from 3:00 PM–6:00 PM Monday–Friday. Haymaker Public House adds $2 off drafts and $6 wells in the same weekday window.

Best Cocktails

The Last Word is A2’s cocktail destination: $9 classics and $5 beer & wine Tuesday through Friday from 5:00 PM–7:00 PM. It’s a smaller room — show up at five if you want a seat at the bar, especially on Thursdays.

The Ravens Club leans more supper-club, with $2 off appetizers during the 4:00 PM–6:00 PM weekday window and a serious whiskey list (130+ pours) once you’re settled. Aventura runs daily from 3:00 PM–6:00 PM with $3 off wine by the glass and $10 martini, Manhattan, or Old Fashioned specials.

Mani Osteria publishes $5 prosecco, $7 Americanos, $8 spritzes, and $4 Peroni from 4:00 PM–6:00 PM Tuesday–Sunday. Lowertown lists $9 margaritas, $4 Two Hearted, and $7 gin & tonics Tuesday–Friday from 4:00 PM–6:00 PM. For a later cocktail window, The Circ Bar runs 6:00 PM–9:00 PM Tuesday–Saturday with discounted cocktails and rooftop patio vibes.

Best for Beer

HopCat Ann Arbor brings $4 local drafts, $6 mules and meowgaritas, and $6 wine from 3:00 PM–5:30 PM — but published days are Monday–Thursday only. With a wide tap list, it’s still the easiest midweek beer crawl stop.

Mothfire Brewing knocks $1 off pints and $2 off flights from 3:00 PM–6:00 PM weekdays if you want a true brewery room. Grizzly Peak Brewing downtown does $1 off house pints, $5 select apps, and $6 wine by the glass from 3:00 PM–6:00 PM Monday–Friday.

South of campus, Zingerman’s Roadhouse runs $2 off all 16oz drafts Monday through Friday from 2:00 PM–6:00 PM — plus BBQ taco and oyster specials. Conor O’Neill’s stretches $5 select pints and $6 Irish coffee from 4:00 PM–7:00 PM Monday–Friday. Late night, Old Town publishes 50% off select drinks Sunday–Thursday from 10:00 PM to midnight, with Monday $1 off Michigan alcohol.

Late & Long Windows

Most Ann Arbor happy hours wrap by 6:00 PM. If you need a longer or later window, these published listings stand out:

  • The Blue Leprechaun — 4:00 PM–8:00 PM Monday–Saturday with $4 Michigan pints and $5 wells
  • The Circ Bar — 6:00 PM–9:00 PM Tuesday–Saturday with discounted cocktails
  • Conor O’Neill’s — stretches to 7:00 PM Monday–Friday
  • The Last Word — 5:00 PM–7:00 PM Tuesday–Friday
  • Old Town — late 10:00 PM–12:00 AM Sunday–Thursday

Pair an early beer stop (Brown Jug, HopCat, or Grizzly Peak) with one of these later rooms instead of trying to chain three 3–6 PM windows on a busy night.

Day-by-Day / Neighborhood Tips

Campus / State Street: Brown Jug owns the early afternoon Monday–Thursday. HopCat is the next stop if you still have time before 5:30 (also Monday–Thursday only). Good Time Charley’s covers Tuesday–Friday campus energy. On football Saturdays, expect everything near campus to be slammed — shift downtown or go earlier.

Main Street downtown: Ashley’s, Grizzly Peak, Jolly Pumpkin, Last Word, Ravens Club, Aventura, and Sava’s all sit in a tight cluster. Monday through Wednesday you can walk two or three; Thursday and Friday, pick a beer spot at three and a cocktail bar at five.

Midweek (Wednesday): Lighter crowds than Friday. Founders-style midweek energy statewide — see our Wednesday happy hours guide for A2 picks that also list late windows.

Thursday: Last Word opens its cocktail window at five — plan around that. Pair with Jolly Pumpkin pizza if you want dinner in the same stretch of downtown.

Friday: Main Street fills early. Ashley’s, Grizzly Peak, Blue Leprechaun, and Circ Bar are safer bets; HopCat and Brown Jug do not publish Friday. Save Last Word for earlier in the week if you hate standing — or see the statewide Friday happy hours guide.

Weekend / late night: Ashley’s Sunday window and Aventura’s daily deal are the safest published Saturday–Sunday anchors. Circ Bar and Blue Leprechaun cover Saturday evenings. Old Town’s late Michigan alcohol deal is the Sunday–Thursday closer after everything else has flipped to full price.

FAQ

When do most Ann Arbor happy hours run?

Most published Ann Arbor windows start between 3:00 PM and 4:00 PM and wrap between 5:30 PM and 7:00 PM. Campus spots like The Brown Jug open earlier (2:00 PM–4:00 PM, Monday–Thursday). Cocktail rooms like The Last Word skew later (5:00 PM–7:00 PM, Tuesday–Friday).

Does HopCat Ann Arbor run happy hour on Friday?

No. Current published listings show HopCat Ann Arbor happy hour Monday–Thursday only, 3:00 PM–5:30 PM. For Friday after-work deals, try Ashley’s, Grizzly Peak Brewing, Jolly Pumpkin, The Blue Leprechaun, or The Circ Bar instead.

Where are the cheapest Ann Arbor happy hours?

The Brown Jug lists $3 Coors/Miller/Labatt 22oz drafts and $5 Long Islands from 2:00 PM–4:00 PM Monday–Thursday. Ashley’s runs $4 Michigan beer pints and $4 wells from 3:00 PM–6:00 PM Monday–Friday and Sunday. The Blue Leprechaun stretches $4 Michigan pints and $5 wells from 4:00 PM–8:00 PM.

What are the best late-night happy hours in Ann Arbor?

The Circ Bar runs 6:00 PM–9:00 PM Tuesday–Saturday with discounted cocktails. The Blue Leprechaun keeps specials going until 8:00 PM. Old Town publishes a late 10:00 PM–12:00 AM window Sunday–Thursday (with Monday $1 off Michigan alcohol).
