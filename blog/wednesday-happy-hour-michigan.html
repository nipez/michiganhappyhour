Thanksgiving Eve (Wednesday, November 25, 2026)

Thanksgiving Eve is one of Michigan’s biggest bar nights — people come home for the holiday and downtown rooms pack earlier than a normal Wednesday. Use this as a planning checklist, not a guarantee of hours:

  • Plan ahead. Pick one or two stops from the lists above and aim for the open. Popular rooms in Detroit, Ann Arbor, Grand Rapids, and Traverse City can fill well before the usual midweek rush.
  • Rideshare or a designated driver. Parking and late traffic get messy on Thanksgiving Eve. Arrange a ride before you start.
  • Call ahead. Holiday-week hours and kitchen cutoffs can change even when a venue normally lists Wednesday. Confirm specials and closing time with the venue the day of.

Treat every deal on this page as a starting point — verify with the venue before you go, especially during holiday week.
