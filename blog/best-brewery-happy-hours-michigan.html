The 15 Best Brewery Happy Hours in Michigan (2026 Guide)

Updated March 2026 · MichiganHappyHour.com

Michigan is one of the top craft beer states in the country, with hundreds of breweries spread from Detroit to the Upper Peninsula. And many of them offer fantastic happy hour deals that make exploring the state's beer scene even more affordable. Here are our picks for the best brewery happy hours in Michigan.

Grand Rapids: Beer City USA

You can't talk about Michigan craft beer without starting in Grand Rapids, which has earned the "Beer City USA" title multiple times. Founders Brewing Co. offers one of the best deals in the city with $2.50 off pints and $5 flatbread pizzas Monday through Thursday from 2–6 PM. The iconic trucking depot taproom is a pilgrimage for any Michigan beer fan.

Archival Brewing is another GR standout — 25% off drinks and 15% off appetizers during happy hour, in a neighborhood taproom that rewards regulars.

Traverse City & Northern Michigan

Up north, the brewery scene punches well above its weight. Rare Bird Brewpub runs happy hour seven days a week with $1 off all pints and half-off apps — one of the most generous deals in the region. Their patio is also one of the best in TC.

No craft beer tour of Northern Michigan is complete without Short's Brewing in Bellaire. The mothership taproom offers $1 off pints and $2 off flights during their 3–5 PM happy hour. Pro tip: try whatever seasonal experimental brew they have on tap.

The Upper Peninsula

Marquette has emerged as a craft beer destination in its own right. Blackrocks Brewery is legendary — the ski-wall patio, cribbage at the bar, and $1 off all beers during happy hour make it the quintessential UP drinking experience. Down the street, Ore Dock Brewing has been crafting Belgian, American, and English ales since 2012 with Lake Superior inspiration.

For something unique, Drífa Brewing Co-op is Michigan's first cooperatively owned brewery, with a dog-friendly patio on the Marquette multi-use path.

West Michigan Coast

On the Lake Michigan coast, Pigeon Hill Brewing in Muskegon offers stunning Muskegon Lake views from their taproom and their Renny IPA is one of the most crushable double IPAs in the state. Happy hour brings $1 off pints and $2 off flights.

Metro Detroit & Southeast Michigan

The Detroit area has its own thriving brewery scene. Batch Brewing in Corktown brings creative small-batch beers with $1 off pints during happy hour, while Tenacity Brewing in Flint — housed in a converted fire station on the Flint River — offers a massive outdoor patio with fire pits and cornhole alongside their craft beers.

Planning Your Brewery Happy Hour Crawl

Michigan's brewery happy hours typically run from 3–6 PM on weekdays, making them perfect for an after-work stop or the start of a weekend evening. Most offer $1–$2 off pints, with flight deals making it easy to sample multiple beers without breaking the bank.
