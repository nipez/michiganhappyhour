Dog-Friendly Happy Hours in Michigan (2026)

Updated August 2026 · MichiganHappyHour.com

After-work drinks are better with a four-legged plus-one. We’ve started tagging venues that welcome dogs (usually outdoors / patio, leash expected) so you can filter the homepage with Dogs or jump straight to the spots below. Policies change — always confirm with the venue, especially for kitchen hours and indoor seating.

Upper Peninsula (Marquette decks)

Portside Inn keeps a dog-friendly deck with a classic after-work window (about 3:00–5:30 PM): $1 off drafts, $5 onion rings, and the famous baked French onion soup. Nautical vibes, 40 years of tradition, and room for a well-behaved pup on the deck.

Drifa Brewing Co-op is Michigan’s first cooperatively owned brewery, with a dog-friendly patio on the Marquette multi-use path. Happy hour runs roughly 3:00–6:00 PM with $1 off pints — an easy stop after a trail walk.

Ann Arbor

Hearsay Brewery calls out dogs allowed on their site. Taproom energy, campus-adjacent pours — ask the bar what’s on special when you arrive, and keep the leash handy for patio seating.

Northeast Michigan

Presque Isle Farm Cider (PIFCider) near Alpena markets itself as dog-friendly. Cider flights + open air make an easy afternoon stop when you’re up the coast — check their board for day-of specials.

The Hive North in Cheboygan welcomes pets. A solid Mackinaw-region pour when you’re between the bridge and the lake; confirm patio rules when you walk in.

West Michigan lakeshore

Fetch Brewing Co in Whitehall is tagged dog-friendly in OpenStreetMap (dog=yes) — the name basically gives it away. Pair a lakeshore run with a pint; ask about today’s specials at the bar.

How to find more

On the homepage, tap the Dogs filter to see every venue we’ve marked dog-friendly. Know a patio we missed? Submit a tip (there’s a dog-friendly checkbox) or claim your listing if you own the spot.
