Best Waterfront & Patio Happy Hours in Michigan (2026)

Updated August 2026 · MichiganHappyHour.com

Michigan’s best happy hours happen on decks overlooking the Great Lakes, harbor marinas, and vineyard hillsides. These statewide picks combine real drink specials with outdoor seating worth planning your afternoon around.

Detroit

Parc Detroit runs 3:00 PM–6:00 PM with $9 craft cocktails. Campus Martius views and craft cocktails — Detroit’s most scenic social hour.

The Skip runs 4:00 PM–6:00 PM with $5 select beers. Outdoor tiki bar hidden in Belt Alley — Detroit’s best-kept happy hour secret.

Traverse City & Leelanau

The Little Fleet runs 4:00 PM–6:00 PM with $5 draft beer. Laid-back outdoor food truck court and bar — a downtown TC institution.

Hop Lot Brewing runs 3:00 PM–5:00 PM with $1 off pints. Top-tier craft beer with vineyard views and West Bay sunsets.

The Cove runs 3:00 PM–5:00 PM with $5 local wines. Fishtown waterfront dining — grab a seat on the deck.

Chateau Chantal runs 4:00 PM–6:00 PM with $6 wine by the glass. Vineyard views overlooking West Bay — hard to beat at golden hour.
