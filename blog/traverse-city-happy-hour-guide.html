Traverse City Happy Hour Guide (2026)

Updated October 16, 2026 · MichiganHappyHour.com

Looking for Traverse City happy hour? This is the editorial playbook: neighborhoods, days of the week, and the 21 spots that currently have verified start/end times in our database. For the live checklist, open /towns/traverse-city. For every published TC listing (including hours TBD), browse /regions/traverse-city.

Downtown Front Street & Union

Most of the walkable core sits on East Front, Union, Cass, State, and Lake. These are the verified windows we’d plan a crawl around:

  • The Little Fleet (448 E Front St) — 4:00 PM–6:00 PM every day. Deals include $5 draft beer, $8 margaritas, $8 negronis, and $8 wine by the glass.
  • Mama Lu’s Taco Shop (234 E Front St) — 3:00 PM–6:00 PM every day with $7 classic margaritas, $5 apps, $4 house wine, and $1 off beer.
  • The Parlor (267 E Front St) — 3:00 PM–7:00 PM Monday–Saturday; 50% off all drinks and 25% off the full kitchen menu, plus all-night happy hour Mondays.
  • Bubba’s (428 E Front St) — 3:00 PM–6:00 PM every day, with a separate late-night deal of $2 well drinks from 9 PM to close.
  • Sorellina (250 E Front St) — 4:00 PM–6:00 PM Monday–Saturday: $5 personal pizzas, $5.50 house wines, $6 bruschetta & caprese.
  • The Flying Noodle (245 E Front St) — 3:00 PM–6:00 PM every day: $5 apps, $4 off signature cocktails, $3 off wine, $2 off drafts.
  • North Peak Brewing (400 W Front St) — 3:00 PM–6:00 PM Sunday–Friday: $1 off all alcohol and $1 off appetizers & pizzas.
  • 7 Monks Taproom (128 S Union St) — 3:00 PM–6:00 PM every day: $5 sliders, $4 dirty fries, $2 off craft drafts.
  • Low Bar (below 7 Monks) — 4:00 PM–7:00 PM Wednesday–Saturday: $8 craft cocktails and a $6 featured old fashioned.
  • Firefly (310 Cass St) — 4:00 PM–7:00 PM every day: $8 craft cocktails, $6 wines, $5 bites menu.
  • Rare Bird Brewpub (229 Lake Ave) — 3:00 PM–5:30 PM every day: $1 off all pints, half-off apps, $5 featured cocktail.
  • Minervas (300 E State St) — lounge happy hour Tuesday–Saturday 4:00 PM–6:00 PM (Sunday lounge specials 2–9 PM noted on the listing).

Old Town, Commons & Boardman Lake

West of downtown toward the Village at Grand Traverse Commons and the Boardman corridor:

  • Left Foot Charley (806 Red Dr) — 4:00 PM–6:00 PM Tuesday–Saturday: $5 wine tastings, cheese plate specials, half-off select bottles.
  • Smoke & Porter (830 Cottageview Dr) — 3:00 PM–6:00 PM Monday–Friday: $2 off craft beers, discounted small plates, $5 well drinks.
  • The Filling Station (642 Railroad Pl) — 3:00 PM–6:00 PM Monday–Friday: $1 off pints, $5 pizza slices, half-off appetizers.
  • Right Brain Brewery (225 E Sixteenth St) — 3:00 PM–6:00 PM Monday–Friday: $4.50 pints and member mug fills.
  • TC Whiskey Co. (201 E Fourteenth St) — 3:00 PM–7:00 PM Monday–Thursday: $6 cocktails (extended until 8 PM on live music nights per the listing).

Bayfront, Grandview & West Bay Shore

  • Lil Bo Bar (313 W Grandview Pkwy) — 3:00 PM–6:00 PM Monday–Friday: $4 wells, $3 domestics, half-off chili & po’ boys.
  • ParkShore Lounge (1401 US 31 N) — 3:00 PM–7:00 PM every day with daily drink specials.
  • Reflect Bistro (255 Munson Ave) — 4:00 PM–7:00 PM every day.
  • The Burrow TC (12930 S W Bay Shore Dr) — 3:00 PM–6:00 PM Tuesday–Saturday: $5 wine, $5 cocktails, $4 beer, rotating small plates.

Patio & outdoor picks

Spots tagged patio / outdoor in our data, or with patio called out in the venue note:

  • The Little Fleet — outdoor food truck court; 4:00–6:00 PM daily.
  • Rare Bird Brewpub — outstanding patio; 3:00–5:30 PM daily.
  • Bubba’s — Front Street patio; 3:00–6:00 PM daily plus late-night wells.
  • Lil Bo Bar — retro patio vibes; weekdays 3:00–6:00 PM.

Happy hour by day of week

Every day: Little Fleet, Mama Lu’s, Bubba’s, Flying Noodle, 7 Monks, Firefly, Rare Bird, ParkShore Lounge, Reflect Bistro.

Monday–Friday only (among verified): Lil Bo Bar, Right Brain, Filling Station, Smoke & Porter. Monday–Thursday: TC Whiskey Co.

Tuesday–Saturday: Left Foot Charley, Minervas lounge, The Burrow TC.

Wednesday–Saturday: Low Bar (4:00–7:00 PM) — best reserved for a midweek or weekend cocktail stop.

Sunday: Daily spots above plus North Peak (Sunday–Friday). Low Bar, Left Foot, Minervas, The Burrow, The Parlor, and Sorellina sit out Sunday on current hours.

Old Mission Peninsula add-ons

Wine-country happy hours live on the peninsula region, not the city town page. Verified windows:

  • Chateau Chantal — 4:00 PM–6:00 PM; $6 wine by the glass, $5 flatbreads.
  • 2 Lads Winery — 4:00 PM–6:00 PM; $5 reserve pours.
  • Bonobo Winery — 4:00 PM–6:00 PM; $5 wine by the glass.
  • Jolly Pumpkin at Mission Table — 3:00 PM–5:00 PM; $2 off sour ales, $5 flatbreads.

Full peninsula list: Old Mission town page and Old Mission region. Leelanau tasting rooms and Suttons Bay stops are under Leelanau Peninsula.

FAQ

How many Traverse City happy hours have verified hours?

We currently list 21 Traverse City spots with verified happy hour start and end times on /towns/traverse-city. The wider Traverse City region page has 68+ published listings, including spots still waiting on confirmed windows.

When do most Traverse City happy hours run?

Most verified TC windows start between 3:00 PM and 4:00 PM and end between 5:30 PM and 7:00 PM. Several Front Street spots run every day, while Low Bar is Wednesday–Saturday 4:00–7:00 PM and Left Foot Charley is Tuesday–Saturday 4:00–6:00 PM.

Where are the best patio or outdoor happy hours in Traverse City?

From our tagged listings and venue notes: The Little Fleet (outdoor food truck court), Rare Bird Brewpub (outstanding patio), Bubba’s (Front Street patio), and Lil Bo Bar (retro patio vibes). Hours and deals are on each spot page.

Are Old Mission Peninsula wineries included?

Old Mission is a separate region. Verified winery and peninsula windows include Chateau Chantal, 2 Lads Winery, and Bonobo Winery (each 4:00–6:00 PM) plus Jolly Pumpkin at Mission Table (3:00–5:00 PM). See /regions/old-mission and /towns/old-mission.
