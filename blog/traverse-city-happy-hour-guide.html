The Ultimate Traverse City Happy Hour Guide (2026)

Updated August 2026 · MichiganHappyHour.com

Traverse City packs more great happy hours into a few walkable blocks than any other town in northern Michigan. With 68 spots listed in our guide, there's something for every mood — from speakeasy cocktails to beachside beers. Here's your complete guide to making the most of happy hour in TC.

Best Overall Deals

The Little Fleet is a TC institution — an outdoor food truck court and bar on East Front Street with $5 draft beers, $8 margaritas, and a wing & beer combo on Wednesday through Sunday. It runs seven days a week and is the perfect casual starting point for any evening downtown.

Mama Lu's Taco Shop is another seven-day-a-week deal with $7 classic margaritas and $5 apps. Their $2 Taco Tuesdays are legendary in TC and one of the best food deals in town, period.

Best Cocktails

For craft cocktails, Low Bar is the move. Hidden beneath 7 Monks Taproom on Union Street, this speakeasy is dark, moody, and serves the best cocktails in TC. Happy hour brings $8 craft cocktails and a $6 featured old fashioned. Get there early on weekends — it fills up fast.

Best Patios

Rare Bird Brewpub has an outstanding patio and runs happy hour all seven days with $1 off all pints and half-off apps. Bubba's on Front Street is another great patio option with a late-night happy hour from 9 PM to close featuring $2 well drinks.

Best for Wine Lovers

Traverse City is the heart of Michigan wine country, so it's no surprise the wine happy hours here are excellent. The wineries on Old Mission Peninsula and Leelanau Peninsula offer some of the most scenic happy hour settings in the state. Chateau Chantal on Old Mission has $6 wines by the glass with vineyard views overlooking West Bay — hard to beat at golden hour.

Day-by-Day Strategy

Most TC happy hours run Monday through Friday from 3–6 PM, but several run all week. Our suggestion: start your week at Rare Bird (Monday, all day vibes), hit Mama Lu's for Taco Tuesday, explore the Little Fleet mid-week, save Low Bar for a Thursday or Friday date night, and cap the weekend with sunset at Chateau Chantal.
