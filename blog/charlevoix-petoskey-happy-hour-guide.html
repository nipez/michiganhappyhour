Petoskey & Charlevoix Happy Hour Guide (2026)

Updated August 2026 · MichiganHappyHour.com

Up north, happy hour means brewery patios overlooking Little Traverse Bay, Hemingway-era steakhouses, and pourhouses with 30 Michigan taps. We’ve got 21 Charlevoix & Petoskey spots mapped — these six have verified hours and deals worth the drive.

Petoskey

Beards Brewery runs 3:00 PM–5:00 PM with $1 off pints. Laid-back downtown Petoskey brewery with a great patio. Monday $3 pints all day is the local move.

Petoskey Brewing runs 3:00 PM–5:00 PM with $1 off all pints. Local brewery with a killer view of Little Traverse Bay.

City Park Grill runs 4:00 PM–6:00 PM with $6 house cocktails. Hemingway’s old haunt — a Petoskey landmark since 1875.

Palette Bistro runs 4:00 PM–6:00 PM with $7 wines. Farm-to-fork dining in the Gaslight District.

Charlevoix

Tap 30 Pourhouse runs 3:00 PM–6:00 PM with $2 off all taps. 30 taps of Michigan craft beer in downtown Charlevoix.

Castle Farms Winery runs 4:00 PM–6:00 PM with $5 tastings. Wine tasting in a stone castle — yes, an actual castle.

How to Plan the Night

Start at Beards or Petoskey Brewing at 3, walk the Gaslight District for City Park Grill cocktails, then drive to Charlevoix for Tap 30 pints. Summer weekends fill patios first — arrive at open.
