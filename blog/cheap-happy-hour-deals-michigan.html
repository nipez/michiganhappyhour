Best Cheap Happy Hours in Michigan (2026)

Updated August 2026 · MichiganHappyHour.com

Happy hour doesn’t have to mean $15 cocktails. These Michigan spots deliver wells under $5, $2 beers, and food deals that actually move the needle — from Detroit dives to UP breweries. Deals change; confirm hours before you go.

Dragonfly runs 5:00 PM–7:00 PM with $5 menu items. Bright and colorful Midtown spot — inclusive $5 happy hour with creative bites.

Zoobie’s Old Town Tavern runs 3:00 PM–6:00 PM with $3-$6 appetizers. Old Town neighborhood tavern — cheap PBR and solid apps in a laid-back setting.
