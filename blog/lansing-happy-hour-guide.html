The Ultimate Lansing Happy Hour Guide (2026)

Updated August 2026 · MichiganHappyHour.com

Capitol after-work and campus weeknights collide in Lansing and East Lansing. Downtown pubs, East Lansing taprooms, and a few longer windows make it easy to plan without guessing. We’ve got 70 Lansing spots mapped — here are the ones with verified hours and specials worth planning around.

Best Overall Deals

HopCat East Lansing runs 3:00 PM–5:30 PM with 50% off select apps, $4 local drafts, $6 mules & meowgaritas. Spartan territory's top beer bar with 100+ taps near campus

Zoobie's Old Town Tavern runs 3:00 PM–6:00 PM with $3-$6 appetizers, $2 PBR, $4 well highballs. Old Town neighborhood tavern — cheap PBR and solid apps in a laid-back setting

One North Kitchen & Bar runs 3:00 PM–7:00 PM with Half-off pizza & appetizers, $4 well drinks, $1 off beers & wine. West side gem with half-off pizza and apps — the best value happy hour in Lansing

Best Near Campus

Sidebar East Lansing runs 3:00 PM–6:00 PM with $5 cocktails, $4 drafts, Bar snack specials. MSU neighborhood sports bar with made-from-scratch food and free parking

Beggar's Banquet runs 4:00 PM–6:00 PM with Half-off drinks, $5 sliders & snacks, Wine Wednesday specials. East Lansing institution with handcrafted cocktails and locally sourced ingredients

Best Downtown / Midtown

Art's Pub runs 3:00 PM–6:00 PM with $4 wells, $1 off drafts, Half-off apps. Legendary Lansing sports bar — best thin-crust pizza in town and ice-cold drafts

Midtown Brewing Co. runs 3:00 PM–6:00 PM with $1 off house pints, $5 apps, $6 cocktails. Downtown Lansing brewpub steps from the Capitol with solid house beers

Cask & Co. runs 3:00 PM–6:00 PM with $5 cocktails, $4 draft beer, $5 select apps. Upscale casual with a great whiskey selection and happy hour specials

FieldHouse runs 4:00 PM–7:00 PM with $5 craft shorts & domestic tallboys, $6 house wine, $7 seasonal cocktails. MSU game-day staple with daily food specials and a solid craft beer lineup

How to Plan the Night

Start East Lansing early (HopCat closes the window at 5:30), then slide downtown for Midtown Brewing or Zoobie's if you still want apps on special. Thursdays are the busiest campus nights — arrive at the open.
