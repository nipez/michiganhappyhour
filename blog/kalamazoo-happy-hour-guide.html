The Ultimate Kalamazoo Happy Hour Guide (2026)

Updated August 2026 · MichiganHappyHour.com

Home of Bell's and a deep craft scene, Kalamazoo packs brewery pints, downtown cocktails, and quirky beer pricing into a compact after-work circuit. We’ve got 30 Kalamazoo spots mapped — here are the ones with verified hours and specials worth planning around.

Best Overall Deals

Bell's Eccentric Cafe runs 3:00 PM–6:00 PM with $1 off Bell's pints, $5 appetizers, Live music nights. Michigan's oldest craft brewery — 20+ taps, a legendary beer garden, and live music

HopCat Kalamazoo runs 3:00 PM–5:30 PM with 50% off select apps, $4 local drafts, $6 mules & meowgaritas. Historic train depot turned beer bar — a short walk from Bell's and Arcadia

Kalamazoo Beer Exchange runs 4:00 PM–6:00 PM with Stock market-style beer pricing, Prices drop when you buy, Market crash specials. Beer prices rise and fall like the stock market — buy low and drink well

Best for Beer

One Well Brewing runs 3:00 PM–6:00 PM with $1 off pints, $2 off flights, Board game library. Community brewery with a huge board game library — beer and games under one roof

Latitude 42 runs 3:00 PM–6:00 PM with $1 off house pints, $5 apps, $6 wine. Spacious brewpub just south of Kalamazoo with house-brewed beers and pub fare

Best Cocktails & Dinner

Principle Food & Drink runs 4:00 PM–6:00 PM with $7 cocktails, $5 draft beer, Half-off select apps. Farm-to-table downtown spot with seasonal menus and expertly mixed cocktails

Stamped Robin runs 4:00 PM–6:00 PM with $8 craft cocktails, $5 beer & wine, Discounted small plates. Stylish cocktail bar downtown with creative drinks and a warm modern vibe

600 Kitchen & Bar runs 4:00 PM–6:00 PM with $6 cocktails, $4 drafts, $5 wine. Friendly downtown gathering spot with fresh, quality food and craft cocktails

Brick and Brine runs 4:00 PM–6:00 PM with $9 mushroom canapé, $9 salmon belly tartare, $9 chicken wings. Upscale brick-fired kitchen with a double-sided fireplace and craft cocktails

How to Plan the Night

Do Bell's or One Well early (3 PM), then HopCat before 5:30 if you want the app discount. Beer Exchange is the fun wildcard when you want prices that move with the room.
