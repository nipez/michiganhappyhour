The Ultimate Bay City Happy Hour Guide (2026)

Updated September 2026 · MichiganHappyHour.com

Looking for happy hour in Bay City, MI? Downtown clusters along Saginaw Street — wine flights at Prost, Michigan beers in a historic hall, and craft cocktails a block away. We currently list 3 Bay City spots with verified hours on /towns/bay-city; here’s how to plan around them.

Wine Bars Downtown

Prost Wine Bar & Charcuterie (1000 Saginaw St) runs 4:00 PM–6:00 PM Tuesday–Friday with $3 off wine flights, $5 featured wines, and charcuterie board specials. Bay City’s upscale wine destination with curated boards and an intimate atmosphere — the brand people search for when they want a polished after-work stop.

Downtown Bars & Historic Spots

Old City Hall (814 Saginaw St) runs 3:00 PM–6:00 PM Monday–Friday with $2 off Michigan beers, $5 appetizer specials, and discounted wine pours. A Bay City staple in a historic setting with a solid Michigan craft beer selection — and the earliest window on this list.

The Public House (901 Saginaw St) runs 4:00 PM–6:00 PM Wednesday–Friday with $2 off craft cocktails, $5 select beers, and shareable plates specials. Upscale vibes meet tiki flair with craft cocktails and even vegan corn dogs — Bay City’s hidden gem between Old City Hall and Prost.

Nearby Tri-Cities (Saginaw & Beyond)

If you’re already in the Tri-Cities corridor, Saginaw is a short hop for longer windows: Merl’s Tavern runs 3:00 PM–7:00 PM with $3 wells, and Harvey’s Grill & Bar runs 3:00 PM–7:00 PM with $2 off cocktails. For the full Midland + Saginaw + Bay City sweep, see our Tri-Cities happy hour guide or browse happy hour in Saginaw.

How to Plan the Night

Start at Old City Hall at 3 for Michigan beers, walk Saginaw Street to The Public House (Wed–Fri) for cocktails at 4, then finish at Prost for wine flights before 6. Want the wider map? Open all 32 Tri-Cities spots, filter Bay City only, or browse the full Michigan guide.
