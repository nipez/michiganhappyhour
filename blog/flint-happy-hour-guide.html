The Ultimate Flint Happy Hour Guide (2026)

Updated August 2026 · MichiganHappyHour.com

Vehicle City's after-work scene spans converted firehouse breweries, longtime taverns, and downtown cocktail spots — with longer windows than you might expect. We’ve got 39 Flint spots mapped — here are the ones with verified hours and specials worth planning around.

Best Overall Deals

Tenacity Brewing runs 3:00 PM–6:00 PM with $1 off all pints, $2 off flights, Food truck specials. Craft brewery in a converted fire station on the Flint River — creative beers and a massive outdoor patio with fire pits

Little Joe's Tavern runs 3:00 PM–7:00 PM with $3 wells, $2 off craft beers, Flight Night specials. A Great Depression-era survivor that outgrew its original spot — Flint's friendliest neighborhood bar with flight nights

Torch Bar & Grill runs 11:00 AM–6:00 PM with $3 domestic drafts, $1 off all burgers, $4 wells. Flint institution since 1931 serving legendary made-to-order burgers in a beloved dive bar setting

Best for Beer

Market Tap runs 3:00 PM–6:00 PM with $1 off all taps, $5 Michigan ciders, Rotating food specials. Flint's rotating local tap room with a chill vibe and strong conversation without the noise

Redwood Steakhouse & Brewery runs 3:00 PM–6:00 PM with $2 off house brews, $6 select steaks, Half-off appetizers. Upscale steaks paired with house-brewed craft beers in a warm Grand Blanc setting

Best Downtown Energy

Churchill's Food & Spirits runs 3:00 PM–7:00 PM with $4 wells, $5 craft drafts, Half-off select appetizers. Reliable downtown spot with daily specials and a loyal following for solid drinks and bar food

The Loft runs 3:00 PM–6:00 PM with $1 off all drinks, Food truck bites below, Patio drink specials. Downtown loft space with early happy hour, food trucks below, an outdoor patio, and big-screen TVs

Soggy Bottom Bar runs 4:00 PM–7:00 PM with $2 off craft cocktails, $5 whiskey pours, Rotating small plates. Modern craft cocktails, an excellent whiskey list, and live music — downtown Flint's freshest bar

X Bar runs 4:00 PM–7:00 PM with $2 off craft cocktails, $5 beer & shot, DJ specials. Sleek downtown cocktail bar with themed nights, game nights, and the best dance floor in Flint

How to Plan the Night

Torch starts absurdly early (11 AM). For a classic after-work loop, hit Tenacity at 3, then Little Joe's or Churchill's if you want wells and apps still on special.
