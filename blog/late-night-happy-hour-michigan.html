Best Late-Night Happy Hours in Michigan (2026)

Updated August 2026 · MichiganHappyHour.com

Most Michigan happy hours end by 6 or 7. These are the spots that keep a window open later — cocktail bars with 5–7 PM starts, late kitchen specials, and rooms that still feel like after-work even after dinner. Here’s where to go when you can’t leave the office at 4.

Detroit (stay out after dinner)

Standby in the Belt runs 5:00 PM–7:00 PM with $8 select cocktails and $5 beer specials — one of the later true happy hour starts downtown.

The Library runs 4:00 PM–7:00 PM with $7 classics and $5 drafts, so you can land after a 6 PM meeting and still catch the window.

The Skip (Belt Alley tiki) runs 4:00 PM–6:00 PM with $5 beers and $6 wells, then stays lively into the night for patio energy after the specials end.

Firebird Tavern and The Block cover Greektown / Midtown with 3–6 and 4–6 PM windows when you want food + drinks before a show that runs late.

Ann Arbor (campus late energy)

The Circ Bar is the late standout: happy hour 6:00 PM–9:00 PM with discounted cocktails and rooftop patio vibes — rare for Michigan.

Black Pearl runs 4:00 PM–6:00 PM with app and drink deals, then carries the night with a big drink list if you’re already downtown.

Traverse City (Front Street after dark)

The Parlor stretches to 7:00 PM with 50% off drinks and kitchen discounts — one of TC’s longer cocktail windows.

Bubba's pairs a 3–6 PM happy hour with late-night well drink specials (9–close) when you want a second act on Front Street.

Right Brain Brewery stays a solid late brewery hang after the 3–6 PM pint deals wrap.

How to use this list

If you need specials after 6 PM, prioritize Circ Bar (A2), Standby / The Library (Detroit), and The Parlor (TC). For everything tagged late-night across the state, browse our late-night happy hour collection.
