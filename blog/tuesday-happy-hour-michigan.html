FAQ

Do most Michigan happy hours run on Tuesday?

Yes. From our live venue data, 169 spots list Tuesday with verified start and end times — usually as part of a Monday–Thursday or Monday–Friday window. Tuesday is also when several rooms publish named specials like Sangria Tuesday, $8 burger & beer Tuesdays, and all-day draft deals.

Which Tuesday happy hours run all day or late?
